Classic French Omelette

  • Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: 1 person
Ingredients Quantity
Eggs 3 large
Unsalted Butter 1 tbsp
Salt and Pepper To taste

Steps:

First, crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk them until no streaks of white remain. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.

  1. Heat the butter in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat.
  2. Pour in the eggs and move them constantly with a spatula.
  3. When the eggs are nearly set but still slightly runny on top, roll the omelette tightly.
  4. Slide it onto a plate and enjoy!
